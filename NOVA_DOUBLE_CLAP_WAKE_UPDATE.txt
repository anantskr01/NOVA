NOVA DOUBLE-CLAP WAKE UPDATE

Added double-clap wake detection to NovaVoiceService.

Behavior:
- Two sharp microphone RMS peaks within about 1.1 seconds wake NOVA.
- A short 180 ms debounce prevents one clap from being counted twice.
- Detection uses SpeechRecognizer's existing onRmsChanged callback, so NOVA does not open a second competing microphone stream.
- On detection, NOVA enters the same wake state as the voice wake phrase, plays the existing wake tone, and shows the existing NOVA wake overlay.
- After waking, the normal voice command window remains active.

Notes:
- Detection is adaptive to the recent microphone noise floor.
- Exact sensitivity depends on the device microphone and Android speech-recognition provider.
- Android background microphone restrictions still apply.
