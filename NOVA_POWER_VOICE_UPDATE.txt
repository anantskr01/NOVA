NOVA POWER + ALWAYS-LISTENING VOICE UPDATE

Changes:
- Camera gesture processing reduced from ~30 FPS to ~15 FPS.
- Requests a 15 FPS camera range when the device exposes a compatible range.
- Reduced fingertip movement gain and increased smoothing/dead-zone to reduce jitter and unnecessary accessibility gestures.
- Increased movement dispatch interval to reduce Android gesture churn.
- Voice service now uses a guarded restart loop so it does not create overlapping SpeechRecognizer sessions.
- Voice service remains a foreground microphone service while hands-free is enabled and automatically restarts recognition after normal results/errors.
- Wake phrase remains: "Hey NOVA", "NOVA", or "NOVA ...".

Important Android limitation:
SpeechRecognizer is session-based. This is a best-effort always-listening mode, not a hardware-level hotword detector. Android may end a recognition session, and the service then restarts it. Continuous microphone use will still consume battery.
