NOVA WAKE EFFECTS UPDATE

Added a lightweight JARVIS-inspired wake experience.

When a wake phrase is recognized:
- NOVA posts an ONLINE/listening notification update.
- A short notification beep is played.
- If Android's Draw Over Other Apps permission is granted, a temporary full-screen transparent HUD appears over the current app/home screen.
- The HUD shows a pulsing NOVA ring, rotating arcs, "NOVA ONLINE", and "VOICE LINK ESTABLISHED" for about 1.45 seconds.
- The overlay is non-touchable and automatically removes itself.

No external graphics or network assets were added. The animation is drawn locally.

IMPORTANT:
Android requires the user to grant "Display over other apps" for the wake HUD to appear while NOVA is not in the foreground. Without that permission, voice wake and the notification still work normally.
